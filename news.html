COMPANY · FDA ANDA · 31 OCT 2026

Kingfriend reports FDA approval of salmon calcitonin injection

Nanjing Kingfriend Bio-Chemical Pharmaceutical said it received the FDA approval letter for its 400 USP Units/2 mL salmon calcitonin injection on 29 October, under ANDA 216996. The company put programme spending at about RMB 49.2 million and said the product is not yet marketed in the United States.

REGULATORY · FDA · 30 OCT 2026

FDA approves tildrakizumab for active psoriatic arthritis

Sun Pharma said the FDA cleared a supplemental biologics licence application for Ilumya (tildrakizumab-asmn) in adults with active psoriatic arthritis, extending a label first granted for plaque psoriasis in 2018. The decision rests on the Phase 3 INSPIRE-1 and INSPIRE-2 studies, which measured response after two 100 mg doses to week 24. The company reported no new safety signals, and the product is approved only for administration by a healthcare professional.

REGULATORY · FDA · 30 OCT 2026

FDA grants accelerated approval to a plasmid DNA immunotherapy for recurrent respiratory papillomatosis

The agency cleared Voceryq (doruxapapogene ralaplasmid and lalikinogene sifuplasmid-gxfd) for adults with the HPV-driven disease, using reduced frequency of surgery as the endpoint. In a 32-patient open-label trial, 7 of 26 patients with at least three prior procedures needed no surgery in the first year. Inovio holds the approval and must run post-marketing studies.

REGULATORY · NMPA · 30 OCT 2026

China's NMPA clears four Medtronic cardiac electrophysiology devices

NMPA approved registration of a magnetically tracked mapping and dual-energy ablation catheter, a cardiac dual-energy ablation system, a three-dimensional mapping system and a single-use body-surface reference electrode, for drug-refractory recurrent symptomatic persistent atrial fibrillation lasting under a year. The agency cited lower risk of complications such as atrial-oesophageal fistula.

REGULATORY · EMA · PRAC 26–29 OCT 2026

EMA safety committee opens Deanxit review and examines immunoglobulin batch reactions

PRAC began a review of Deanxit (flupentixol/melitracen) after reports of movement and cognitive disorders that in some cases persisted after stopping treatment. It is also assessing a signal of increased hypersensitivity reports with specific batches of human normal immunoglobulin, after recalls of Flebogamma DIF, Yimmugo and Gamunex batches in some member states. No conclusions yet.

QUALITY · MHRA · 29 OCT 2026

MHRA recalls a lercanidipine batch after a strength mix-up

Accord-UK is recalling batch M2510877 of lercanidipine hydrochloride 10 mg tablets after a sealed carton was found to hold one blister of 10 mg and one blister of 20 mg tablets. The Class 2 recall operates at pharmacy and wholesaler level; the agency said 20 mg sits inside the authorised dose range and that the risk to patients is low.

REGULATORY · FDA · 26 OCT 2026

FDA approves talazoparib with enzalutamide in HRR-mutated prostate cancer

The combination is cleared for adults with metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naïve or -sensitive prostate cancer carrying homologous recombination repair gene mutations. In the 599-patient TALAPRO-3 trial, median radiographic progression-free survival was not reached versus 45.8 months on enzalutamide plus placebo, a hazard ratio of 0.48. Overall survival data were immature at the analysis. The review ran under Project Orbis with the TGA and Health Canada.

QUALITY · MHRA · 26 OCT 2026

MHRA recalls Xeal Pharma clopidogrel oral liquids after potency shortfall

A Class 2 patient, pharmacy and wholesaler recall covers all batches of Xeal Pharma's unlicensed clopidogrel oral solution and suspension, which a formulation error left at 76% of labelled content. Pharmacists must quarantine stock and contact patients, including anyone dispensed the product from 20 July 2026 where batch records are unavailable.
